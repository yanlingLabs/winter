# The Winter island

*Read this before touching `apple/Winter/Sources/Island/`, `packages/core/src/island/`, the hello's `capabilities`/`tty`, `island.pending`, or where an approval, question or desktop-switch prompt shows on the Mac.*

A Dynamic-Island-style surface at the Mac's camera housing, in Winter.app. Phase 1: the slim strip, the cards born from the housing, the open island with its tab mechanism and **Needs you**. Phase 2 (below): the tabs — Sessions, Shelf, Usage and limits, Devices, Watches, Servers — and the strip's working count ("The tabs"); the computer-use pill and its mirror, and turn-result cards ("Computer use, results, Watches").

## Two forms

- **Cards on top** — quick, ephemeral, born from the housing with the desktop-switch pill's liquid (`DesktopIslandFluid`, `WinterCUPresentation`, made public for this): approvals, questions and URL link requests as **rounded rectangles** (`IslandRectShell` → `CUIslandLiquidFrame` with `DesktopIslandGeometry.bodyCornerRadius`), view requests (the desktop switch, and future ones) as **pills** (the package's own `DesktopIslandView`, same look and words).
- **The island** — the permanent surface. A **slim strip** extending the housing, STATIC. It never covers a menu-bar click it does not need (`IslandLayout.strip(_:waiting:)`): while NOTHING waits it is exactly the housing (black on black, nothing drawn; a click there — where no menu item can be — still opens the island), and on a screen without a housing there is no strip at all (an empty island has nothing to show; this blocks no menu click, which a minimal top-edge target could). While something waits it grows its wings (the housing's width plus 46 pt each side, its height) with the amber "waiting for you" icon on the left and the count of every pending card on the right — or, without a housing, a 112 pt tab hanging from the top centre at the menu bar's height. Only then do the wings or the tab take clicks. A **click** on it opens the island (ONE fixed size, 560 × 168 pt, for every tab — "The open island's shape" below — morphing out of the strip, "The open morph") — on **Needs you** from the left (waiting) wing, on **Sessions** from the right (working) wing, on the tab it was on from the housing (`IslandLayout.stripPart` → `IslandStripPart.action`, pure, `IslandOpenMorphTests`; an empty wing counts as the housing; while the island is open the strip lies under its top band, which maps a click to the same parts — a wing whose tab is not showing switches to it, anything else closes; VoiceOver has the same as named actions); a click outside (global + local mouse-down monitors) or **Esc** closes it (the panel's own key handler when it is key; a global key-down monitor otherwise, which macOS delivers only to an app trusted for Accessibility). A click on the island's top band closes it too. **Hover (user 2026-10-17):** the strip — never the open island — glows DARK while the pointer is over it: the Dispatch pill buttons' two-layer glow (`PillHoverGlowMetrics`) in black, 0.15 s ease-out. Its panel is the strip plus `stripGlowMargin` (14 pt) at the sides and below, transparent until it glows; the hosting view's `.activeAlways` tracking area over the strip itself drives it (`IslandStripHover`), event-driven — nothing runs while the pointer is elsewhere.
- **Tabs**: `IslandTabRegistry.tabs` in swipe order — **Needs you, Sessions, Shelf, Usage and limits, Devices, Watches, Servers** (`IslandTabsTests` pins it). A tab is added by naming it there and giving `IslandTabBody` (`IslandTabViews.swift`) a body for its id, plus a refresh in `IslandTabModels.refresh(tab:)`. A **two-finger horizontal swipe** while the pointer is over the open island changes tab (`TrackpadHorizontalSwipeRecognizer`, the Dispatch pill's; fingers left = next, no wrap); small dots show where you are, the selected tab's title on the left.
- **Needs you** lists every pending card, newest first, each answerable there: approvals, questions and links with the transcript's own `TranscriptInteractionCard` (rule options, multi-question decks and notes included), view requests with ✕ / ✓.

## Routing (`IslandRouter`, pure; `IslandRoutingTests`)

1. **Dispatch, or a Dispatch-spawned child** (mode `dispatch` — Dispatch's own cards, now that it respects its approval policy —, origin `dispatch-child`, a coordinator among the known Dispatch sessions, or a card mirrored onto one): through the Dispatch pill while it is visible (the pill morphs into the card — [dispatch-and-messaging](./dispatch-and-messaging.md#the-mac-pills-cards-approvals-questions-link-requests)); on top while it is not. The pill's visibility change re-routes.
2. **Any other session**: HIDDEN on top while that session's host is in front; on top otherwise.
   - Winter.app: the app window or a detached window showing the session is key or main AND Winter is frontmost (`AppWindowController.isKeyOrMain`, `DetachedWindowController.isKeyOrMain`).
   - A terminal (the CLI/TUI): only its EXACT terminal window/tab, through its tty (below). No exact match → shown. A phone host (role `remote`) never hides anything.
3. **View requests** (`prompt.desktopVisit`'s card): ALWAYS on top.

Every pending card is in Needs you and the strip's count whatever its placement. Re-routing is event-driven: an app activation, a Winter window becoming key/main, the app (de)activating, the pill showing/hiding, and — only while a card is hidden behind a terminal — an `AXObserver` on that terminal app's focused-window/title changes (`TerminalFocusWatcher`; nothing installed otherwise).

## The terminal match (`TerminalWindowMatcher`, `TerminalWindowMatcherTests`)

- The CLI sends its controlling terminal in its hello (`HelloParams.tty`, `packages/cli/src/tty-name.ts`: the device number of the first terminal among stdin/stdout/stderr matched against `/dev/ttys<minor>`, else a `/dev` scan; no process spawned). The daemon keeps it on the connection's `HubClient` and lists it in `island.pending`'s `hosts`.
- The app reads the process table (`sysctl KERN_PROC_ALL`: pid, parent, `e_tdev` → `devname`, `p_comm`) and, only with Accessibility trust, the frontmost app's focused window title and selected tab title (`AXTabGroup`'s selected tab; 0.1 s messaging timeout, read-only, nothing set) — off the main thread, only when a card's routing needs it.
- The window is read only when a target tty belongs to the front app's process tree — otherwise the answer is "not in front" with no accessibility call at all (which would also switch on a Chromium app's AX tree). Re-judgings are one serial pump in `IslandStore` (fetches and reroutes coalesced; a judgement read while a newer ask came in is read again, never applied — `generation`).
- POSITIVE evidence only: the tty must be on a process descending from the frontmost app (else not in front); the app must have a focused window on this Space (AX, or the window server's on-screen list without trust — minimised, another Space or none → `unknown`); then (1) a title naming a tty names exactly one; (2) in a DEDICATED terminal app (`dedicatedTerminals`: Terminal, iTerm2, Ghostty, kitty, WezTerm, Alacritty, Warp, Hyper, Rio, Tabby) one terminal = that one; (3) there, the title's PROCESS segment (`processSegment`: the one before the size in Terminal.app's "folder — winter — 80×24", or a whole unsegmented title; never the folder) naming the program running in exactly one of its terminals. Anything else is `unknown` → the card shows.
- **Limits**: Terminal.app/iTerm2 titles carry no tty by default, so with several tabs at a shell prompt the answer is `unknown`; iTerm2's sessions are children of its server daemon, not of the app (`unknown`/`notFront` → shown); tmux/ssh: the CLI's pty belongs to a server (shown); an editor's integrated terminal needs the tty in a title; no Accessibility trust → only the process-tree and single-terminal rules.

## Queue (`IslandQueue`, pure; `IslandQueueTests`, `IslandStoreTests`)

- Cards do not stack: a new card REPLACES the current one, which compacts.
- The card being TYPED INTO (its panel took the keyboard for a click in its text field) is held exactly like a hovered one — never replaced, never compacted, never hidden by a re-route — so no keystroke can land in the app behind. The keyboard goes back (`IslandPanel.rest()`, restoring the app the user was in) only when that card is answered (here or anywhere), on an explicit dismiss (a click on the waiting pill) or on Esc; a click in another window simply lets go.
- While the current card is HOVERED or typed into (or is a view request counting down), the newcomer waits as a small compact pill just below it (`IslandWaitingPill`); it expands when the current one clears or self-compacts, or when the user CLICKS it — which tucks the current one away (compacted). A waiting view request is never displaced by an approval.
- **View requests are never compacted** (`IslandQueueTests`, a randomised invariant included): their default-allow runs whether anything draws them or not. A click on the waiting pill under one swaps it into the waiting slot (still drawn, still counting) instead of tucking it away; a view request keeps the waiting slot over an approval (which compacts); a second view request waits its turn in `IslandQueue.viewQueue` (oldest first — the older stays on top or waiting), expanding or taking the waiting slot as the one before it is answered. One in line is not drawn, so it is not acked (`IslandStore.viewRequestsOnTop` is the expanded and waiting ones only): the helper's own prompt shows it after `ISLAND_SHOWN_ACK_MS`.
- **Self-compaction**: approvals, questions and links stay expanded `IslandQueue.selfCompactAfter` (6 s); unhovered approvals, questions and links (never view requests) compact into the strip's left wing (the amber symbol and its count — NOTHING else is drawn for them: no badge under the housing, user ruling 2026-10-17) and the Needs-you list. A pointer leaving a card past its time gives it `hoverLeaveGrace` (1.5 s). They stay pending until answered anywhere; nothing re-expands a compacted card on top — a click on the waiting wing opens the island on Needs you, where each is answered. (Why a click and not a hover, which the old badge used: the wing sits IN the menu bar's row, where the pointer passes all the time on its way to the menus and status items — a hover would pop a card out by accident, compact it again 6 s later, and pop it again on the next pass; a click is deliberate and is the strip's existing gesture, so it needs no monitor of its own.)
- View requests never self-compact: their deadline (the card's `expiresAt`, default-allow) is the answer.
- A card hidden behind its host leaves the top; when it comes back it goes straight to compacted (the strip's count and Needs you) if it was on top before (no second pop). A card answered from the island goes back up into the housing; one answered elsewhere just goes.

## The desktop switch moved into the island

- The app's hello advertises `CLIENT_CAPABILITY_ISLAND` (`"island"`, `packages/protocol/src/methods.ts`) on its MAIN feed connection (`AppModel.clientCapabilities` → `WinterClient.setClientCapabilities`, re-sent on EVERY reconnect).
- While any island client is connected, `islandAwareDesktopPanel` (`packages/core/src/computer-use/wiring.ts`) does not ask the helper for `prompt.desktopVisit`; the island shows the session's own card (`approval_requested` with `toolName: "ComputerV2"`, `onTimeout: "allow"` and the `switch` option — told apart by that shape, never by its words; `DesktopVisitCardWords` reads the app, bundle id and reason back out of the daemon's sentence only to fill the line). ✓ = `approval.respond {approved: true, optionId: "switch"}`, ✕ = `approved: false`. The first answer wins in the broker across the transcript card, the Dispatch pill, the island and the helper.
- The helper's panel stays the FALLBACK, with the card's own deadline and its answer chained: at a prompt with no island client; when the last island client LEAVES before an answer (a quit or crash mid-prompt — `IslandRegistry.onAllClientsGone`); and when no island client says it shows the card within `ISLAND_SHOWN_ACK_MS` (2 s) — the app sends `island.shown {ids}` for the view requests whose words are actually VISIBLE — a card on top once its words have faded in (`IslandController.cardWordsIn`, 0.95 s), the waiting pill as it is drawn, the open island's Needs you once its morph has faded the contents in —, so a Winter.app that is connected but HUNG still gets the prompt on screen (then possibly both show; the first answer wins). `close()` closes whichever is up. `packages/core/test/island/registry.test.ts` pins every leg.

## Daemon (`packages/core/src/island/registry.ts`)

- `IslandRegistry` observes every appended event (`hub.addObserver`): `approval_requested`/`question_asked`/`elicitation_requested` add a card (payload = the event), their resolutions remove it; a Dispatch MIRROR (`childSessionId`) folds into the child's own card (answered at the child); `harness_attached`/`_detached` on a session with a card re-notify. Pending-ness is the BROKERS' (`ApprovalBroker.pendingMeta`, `QuestionBroker.isPending`, `ElicitationBroker.urlFor`): a card the broker no longer holds is pruned at the next list. ≤ 256 held.
- `island.shown {ids}` (harness only): the client shows these cards — the desktop-switch prompt's ack (above).
- `island.pending` (harness role only — not in `REMOTE_ALLOWED_METHODS`, not for plugins) → `{items: [{sessionId, answerSessionId, kind, event, session: {mode, origin, parentSessionId, title}, hosts: [{clientName, role, tty}]}]}`. An island client is told `island.changed` (a bare JSON-RPC notification) once per burst (one microtask): params `{}` when the pending set (or a pending session's hosts) changed — the app then asks again — and `{working: true}` (plus `pending: true` when both happened) when a turn started or ended anywhere (`turn_started`/`turn_completed`): the app re-lists `session.list` for the strip's working count (`IslandCoalescedRun`: one list in flight, one more after a burst). Nothing is sent while no island client is connected; nothing runs while idle. No `SessionEvent` changed: no fixture, no Swift mirror, no remote allowlist.
- `island.hosts` (harness only) → `{sessions: [{sessionId, hosts}]}` for every session with an attachment (`SessionHub.attachedSessionIds`/`attachedHarnesses`): the Sessions tab's "where is it" — `session.list` carries no hosts.
- `island.usage {sessionId?}` (harness only) → `IslandUsageRegistry.snapshot` (`packages/core/src/island/usage.ts`): `windows` — the latest `rate_limit_event` per provider and window kind since the daemon started, each tagged with the reporting session's `provider` (the usage tab's Codex fallback reads the `codex-oauth` ones, [usage](./usage.md)) (the projector's `onRateLimit` dep hands the frame's `rate_limit_info` over; the frame stays unpersisted; the SDK emits it only for subscription-shaped quota, so a home that never hits one has none); `subscription` — `QuotaManager.subscriptionQuota()`; `background` — `quota.state`'s numbers (Winter's background jobs, NOT the user's sessions); `session` — that session's summed `turn_completed` tokens since start (0 for a provider that reports no usage), its last `contextTokens` (sent only for a single-round turn) and its model's catalog `contextWindow` (only for an explicit model tag the catalog knows). Nothing estimated. `packages/core/test/island/usage.test.ts` + the socket leg in `registry.test.ts`.
- Neither is remote-reachable nor a `SessionEvent`: no fixture, no Swift mirror, no allowlist change.
- Swift side: `WinterKit/WinterClient+Island.swift` (`islandPending()`, `islandHosts()`, `islandUsage(sessionId:)`, `capabilityIsland`, `islandChangedNotification`), `IslandClientTests`.

## The app (`apple/Winter/Sources/Island/`)

- `IslandStore` (state, routing applied to the queue, answers through the app's client to the card's ANSWER session — the island never attaches to a session), `IslandController` (panels, pointer, open/close, the one wake-up), `IslandViews`, `IslandLayout`, `IslandPanel` (non-activating, `.statusBar` level, every Space, `sharingType .none`, never key EXCEPT when a click lands in a text field — a question's "Other" — which makes it key for that very click; `rest()` gives it back), `IslandGallery` (offscreen renders). Wired in `AppDelegate.startIsland` (never under unit tests, never without a token).
- Look: the floating cards wear the black shell, hairline rim, shadow and morph blur of the Dispatch pill (`CUIslandLiquidFrame`) — the open island does not (pure black, no rim: "The open island's shape" below); its typed text (`Typography.composerField()`, 14 pt — light on the dark shells) for every card's main line (a tab row's title is `IslandQuietRow.titleFont`, "Island visual language" below), the white ✕ / blue ✓ circles with `pillHoverGlow`. Every card's answer layout is ONE (`IslandAnswerLine`): the words, then ✕ and ✓ (↗ for a link) at the trailing end, bottom-aligned with the last line — on top, in Needs you, like the turn result and the view-request pill; a link says its host once, with a lock, beside ↗ (the header names the session and the server). Pointer through mouse-move monitors (global + local) installed only while a card or the open island is on screen; outside what is drawn the top panel ignores the mouse.
- **Hover** (`IslandHover`, `HoverGrowth`; `IslandOpenMorphTests`, `HoverGrowthTests`): the controllers decide every panel's hover (`IslandHover.isOver`: the shape, or the GROWN shape while hovered), so the hit area, the hold and the grow agree, and they publish the pointer in each panel's own unscaled space (`IslandHover.unscaled`, `islandPanelSpace()`), so a row's or button's hover sits under the pointer at any grow. Every FLOATING panel — a card on top (approval, question, link, view request), the waiting pill, the turn result, the computer-use pill and the in-island mirror — grows from its TOP edge (the housing's side) on the pill's spring by the SAME POINTS as the compact Dispatch pill (user ruling 2026-10-17: a relative 3 % made a big card grow far more than the pill): `HoverGrowth` (`WinterCUPresentation`, pinned to `DispatchPillMetrics.compactWidth`/`hoverScale`) — the width grows by 316 × 3 % = **9.48 pt**, the height in proportion (a uniform scale `1 + 9.48 / width`: no stretched text; the compact pill stays exactly 1.03). So a 400 pt island card grows 2.37 %, the 380 pt mirror 2.49 %, the 340 pt computer-use pill 2.79 %, the 280 pt waiting pill 3.39 %. **The open island itself has no hover grow** (user 2026-10-17); its tabs' rows and tiles follow the island's visual language (below, user 2026-10-18 and 2026-10-19) — no surface, the title lifts and the details and actions appear — while Needs you's options and the Usage tab's sections below its cost block still lighten and glow softly (`IslandHoverSurface`, the pills' glow radii, white). Not grown: the strip (the housing's own silhouette, static — growing it would bulge past the hardware notch) and the detached mirror (a movable, resizable window whose content fills its frame: a grow would be clipped and would fight a drag). A test sweeps `Sources/Island` for any other `scaleEffect` or a literal factor (a title's lift goes through `hoverLiftLabel`, in `Sources/Usage/HoverLine.swift`).
- **Surfaces**: cards on top `IslandLayout.cardCorner` (24) and `cardPadding` (16); the mirrors (in the island and detached) `mirrorCorner` (24) with the picture concentric inside a 12 pt inset; Needs you's link and view-request cards on the transcript card's own surface (`islandInnerCard()`: `CardSurface`, a `HairlineElevated` rim, 28 pt) beside the transcript's cards; every row and tile `rowCorner` (16), `rowGap` (8) apart, `listInset` (14) from the island's edge (Needs you's cards 12 apart, each with its header); the header and the card headers 20 pt in; the tabs' rows (`IslandQuietRow`, no surface) 20 pt in too, under the header's title, `IslandQuietRow.rowGap` (6) apart. The open island's header (`IslandOpenHeader`): the tab's title (Needs you with its count) and, on the same centre line, the tab dots — the selected one a short bar, sliding on a spring.
- **Screen**: the screen with a camera housing, else the main screen (`IslandLayout.islandScreen`) — a fixed place, re-read when displays change (the helper's own desktop-switch pill uses the pointer's screen instead).
- **Idle**: the strip is a static view; no timer or display link runs. Display-rate frames only while a shape moves (`TimelineView(.animation)` until `DesktopIslandFluid.settle`, or the open morph's `IslandOpenMorph.settle`); the queue's self-compaction is ONE `DispatchWorkItem` while a card is expanded; the open morph has ONE (settled, or back in the strip); no ticker for a view request (its expiry arrives as `island.changed`); a tab list's edge fades are judged on scroll only (`onScrollGeometryChange`).
- **The open island's shape** (`IslandEdgeShape`, `IslandOpenFrame`; `IslandOpenMorphTests`): pure black, an extension of the hardware housing — a flat top flush with the screen's edge, a concave **shoulder** (`IslandLayout.islandShoulder`, 14 pt, a quarter circle as one cubic) at each top corner flaring OUT into that edge past the body, straight sides and continuous 32 pt bottom corners; one path, so no seam. A click on a shoulder is a top-band click (`onTopBand`: anything but a wing closes). No rim (a light line would outline it against the housing); its soft drop shadow (18 pt, 6 down) is masked to start at the housing's height and fade in over 28 pt, so nothing darkens the menu bar along its top edge or its shoulders. With or without a housing it hangs from the top edge the same way. (The cards, the waiting pill, the computer-use pill and mirror and the turn result KEEP their rim and shadow: they rest 10 pt below the housing (`DesktopIslandGeometry.gap`), floating by design — a drop that parted from it — so the rim never sits against the housing's edge, and it is what sets them off against a dark window.) The strip has never had a rim (`IslandStripView` is a black fill) and is unchanged — no shoulders on it: idle it is exactly the housing, black on black, and ears beside the hardware would paint into the menu bar.
- **One fixed size** (`IslandLayout.island`, `islandWidth` × `islandHeight` = 560 × 168, user 2026-10-17: ~2/5 of the first 420): every tab, whatever it lists — the island is never re-measured or re-laid out on a tab change or new content (a screen too short for it clamps the height, the same for every tab there). 560 is the island family's widest (the typing Dispatch pill, the desktop-switch pill's ceiling). **The header sits inline with the housing** (user 2026-10-17): with a housing, the title (and Needs you's count) is left of it and the tab dots right of it, in the housing's own band (`IslandOpenHeader(height: topInset, housingWidth:)`), so all of the island under the housing is the tab's list; without a housing the header is its own 46 pt row under the top band. Each tab's body scrolls inside (`IslandTabScroll`): a vertical scroll view with overlay scrollers (shown only while scrolling, whatever the system preference — `IslandOverlayScrollers`), its top and bottom edges fading softly (18 pt) only where more of the list lies beyond them (`IslandScrollEdges`, judged on scroll); the Shelf's grid scrolls the same way; empty states sit centred. **Scroll vs swipe** (`IslandScrollAxisLock`, `IslandTabsTests`): a two-finger gesture's DOMINANT axis decides once — after 12 pt of travel and only when one axis leads by 1.5× (a diagonal start stays undecided) — and holds through its momentum until the next gesture: vertical goes on to the list (the swipe recognizer is reset), horizontal goes to `TrackpadHorizontalSwipeRecognizer` (one tab per gesture) and everything else of it is swallowed — its momentum, and any vertical turn the recognizer ignores (`passesOn`), so the list never moves under a swipe; a mouse wheel (no phases) always scrolls. The overlay scrollers are put back when the system's preferred style changes (`NSScroller.preferredScrollerStyleDidChangeNotification`).
- **The open morph** (`IslandOpenMorph`, pure; `IslandOpenModel`; `IslandOpenMorphTests`): the black mass grows out of the strip as it is drawn at the click (the housing alone while nothing waits or works; the strip's wings when they show; the tab without a housing) into the open island — ONE shape whose width, height, bottom corners (strip 10 → island 32) and top shoulders (strip 0 → island 14, spreading with the width) reshape continuously on critically damped springs (width a touch ahead of height: it spreads along the housing, then drops), so the size never overshoots or bounces. On EVERY frame its top is flush with the screen's edge (y = 0) and, past the click's first frame (the strip itself), flares into it through its shoulders — a test samples 120 frames from the housing, the wings and the no-housing tab. The contents (laid out at the island's resting size, clipped to the shape) fade and un-blur in with the cards' morph blur only once the shape has mostly grown (from 0.2 s; at rest by 0.5 s). Closing plays it backwards 1.6× faster — the contents go first, then the mass shrinks back into the strip — and the panel is ordered out when it is there; a reopen mid-way continues from where the shape is. Frames (`TimelineView(.animation)`) only while it moves; Reduce Motion: no growth, it appears and goes (shoulders included).
- Esc's global key-down monitor exists only while the island is open (removed on close). The view-request pill's VoiceOver label carries no seconds (nothing keeps them current here): "… Winter switches by itself if you don't answer."
- Offscreen renders: `IslandGalleryTests` with `TEST_RUNNER_WINTER_ISLAND_RENDER_DIR=<dir>` (the strip, each card, a card's birth, the waiting pill, the compacted state — the strip alone —, the open island, its morph as an 8-frame filmstrip; and `IslandGallery.renderShape`, on a LIGHT desktop where an outline or a shadow line at the edge would show: every tab at the fixed size, the morph from the housing, the wings and without a housing, the strip, a card at rest beside the same card hovered); `ImageRenderer` draws AppKit-backed controls (a text field) blank, and no scroll view (the gallery lays a tab out flat, cut and faded at the island's edge).

## Island visual language (user 2026-10-18)

**Shared language, own shapes** (user 2026-10-19). The usage plan block (`UsagePlanSlotCard`, `UsagePlanLine` — [usage](./usage.md), "Plan lines, revised") sets the LANGUAGE every tab speaks; each tab keeps the SHAPE that fits its own data. (A first pass put the plan lines' bar on every row — status bars on Sessions, all-or-nothing lines on Devices, uptime bars on Servers; the user: "i didnt meant to copy it. but to understand its style and try to make something that fits together.") The shared pieces: `Sources/Island/IslandQuietRow.swift` — `IslandQuietRow` (the measures), `IslandLiftRow` (a row: its mark, its title with muted words beside it, what trails the title, the lines under it), `IslandRowWords` (a muted line, with what trails it), `IslandStatusMark` / `IslandStatusWord` (a dot, a hollow dot or a glyph, with a word), `IslandChip` (the plan header's "Pro" chip: a port, a link), `IslandRowActionButton` (Stop and its phases, the chips' size) — and `Sources/Usage/HoverLine.swift` — `HoverLift` (the plan lines' measures, pinned equal to them), `HoverLiftReader` (a unit's hover), `hoverLiftLabel` (a title's lift; outside `Sources/Island`, so the island's no-`scaleEffect` sweep holds), `HoverSegmentLine` (the cost split). `HoverLineTests`, `IslandQuietTabsTests`. A new tab follows these rules:

1. **No boxes.** No card, fill, border or tile behind a row, a file or a group: content floats on the island's black.
2. **Typography carries the hierarchy.** A clear title — white, `IslandQuietRow.titleFont` (`Typography.label(.medium)`, one step above the plan names' caption) — over muted words (`Theme.textMuted`, the tiny step); figures in the light `Typography.tinyDigits(.regular)`. Every row starts at the usage block's (and the header's) left edge, `IslandQuietRow.inset`, its mark in one column (`markWidth`), so every tab's titles start at one x; trailing words end at the plan lines' right edge.
3. **One accent per source, used sparingly.** `UsagePlanColor` (Codex green, Claude Code clay, Antigravity blue; Winter's `Theme.accent` for anything else) on a session's dot and the cost split; amber (`pillReviewAmber`) ONLY for what needs you; a status may wear a semantic system colour (green running or connected, red an error or a force quit). Everything else is white or grey.
4. **Quiet at rest, revealed on hover.** At rest a row is what you need to scan — usually one line; a second line shows at rest only when it says something worth scanning (a session needs you or failed, the helper's permissions, a status that could not be read, a Stop that failed). Under the pointer the unit LIFTS: its title grows ×1.12 from its leading edge (`hoverLiftLabel`, `IslandQuietRow.liftPadding` makes its room) and its details and actions appear under it. `HoverLiftReader` reads the island's pointer (its panels are never key) and `.onHover` (a window) and sets what is shown inside `withAnimation(HoverGrowth.animation)` — the Dispatch pill's spring — so a row grows on it and what is below GLIDES rather than jumps. A row's actions stay on screen while one is in the middle of something (`IslandQuietRow.showsActions`: a Stop waiting for its second click, stopping, a force quit offered, a Stop that failed). VoiceOver never hovers: each row is one element whose label carries every word the row shows only under the pointer and whose actions are its buttons (Stop with the same two clicks, each link, a watch's Stop).
5. **One honest shape per kind of data — a line ONLY for a real amount.** The two lines left are the cost split by model (the Usage tab's **Models** line) and a watch's time left. Everything else takes the shape that fits it: a status dot (a session, a device, a server), a glyph, a logo, a small label, a chip. Never a decorative bar that encodes nothing.

- **computerUse**: a session is listed from its first `computer_v2`/`ComputerV2` `tool_call` in a turn (any thread) to its main `turn_completed` — never per script, so the pill does not blink while the model thinks between scripts. `running` = a script in flight; `title` and `apps` (≤ 3 names: `apps.open` names, bundle ids and `.app` paths shown as names, `browsers.open` hosts) from the latest script; a script naming no app keeps the turn's apps. - **results**: a main `turn_completed` with `end_turn` and some main-thread assistant text is held `ISLAND_RESULT_SETTLE_MS` (1.5 s, one unref'd timer, only while an island client is connected); a main `turn_started` meanwhile (a queued message, a fold, a loop's next turn) cancels it. Never for `aborted`/`error`, a silent turn, a turn started by an automated door (`ISLAND_NO_RESULT_ORIGINS`: `messaging`, `dispatch` — Dispatch reports its children itself —, `workflow` — a Workflow `agent()` child's `runTurn` —, `routine` — a scheduled routine —, `embedded-probe`), any turn of a `workflow-child` or routine (`routine`, `routine/<id>`) session, or a session with a card still pending. Dispatch's `dispatch-wake` turn and a `computer-wake` turn ARE shown. Summary: markdown marks dropped, one line, ≤ 240 chars. One per session (its next turn removes it), ≤ 32 held, 10 min TTL.
- **watches**: the wake registry's `listAll()` (`ComputerUseControl.wakesList`) — every armed trigger with `armedAt`, labels only (never the model's note); `wake_armed`/`wake_ended` only trigger the notice. Stop = the existing `computerUse.wakes.cancel`.
- **usageAlarms**: every plan alarm that FIRED and whose notice no client has acknowledged ([usage](./usage.md), "Plan alarms") — read from usage.db at every list (`deps.usageAlarms` → `UsageService.firedAlarms`), so they survive a restart and wait for an app that is away: `{id, provider, windowKey, kind, scope?, windowMinutes?, thresholdPercent, usedPercent, resetsAt?, at (when it fired)}`. No session behind them. A fire or an acknowledgement (`usage.alarms.ack`) is told as `island.activityChanged` (`UsageService`'s `onAlarmNotices`, late-bound in `daemon.ts` to `usageAlarmsChanged`).

### The app (`IslandActivityStore`, `IslandActivityController`)
- **One slot under the housing**, its own panel (`IslandPanel`, `.statusBar`, never key, `sharingType .none`), born and retreating with phase 1's liquid (`DesktopIslandFluid`); at rest the shell is `IslandMorphShell` (same black, rim, shadow, and the compact pill's 9.48 pt hover grow — `HoverGrowth`). **Precedence** (`IslandActivityArbiter`, pure): the open island, or a card needing an answer expanded or waiting on top → the slot is empty (the CU pill and its mirror YIELD — hidden, the mirror's capture stopped — and a result is HELD, born when the top frees within 30 s, else dropped); else a turn result; else the CU pill (or its mirror). **Rung 4** (the cursor's `foreground` on, until off): the slot is empty and every detached mirror goes alpha 0 and click-through, so the agent's hardware click never meets Winter (the helper's `CUHitTest` skips alpha-0 windows and refuses `winterItself` under any other Winter window). At rest the panel is cut to what it draws plus 22 pt of shadow (a closing mirror's area is kept 0.5 s for its spring), so no transparent Winter area sits over the menu bar or the app below. `IslandController` tells the slot after every redraw of its top (`onTopChanged`) and every focus change (`onFocusChanged`); `IslandQueue`/`IslandStore`'s logic is untouched (the store only holds `activity`).
- **The CU pill** (`IslandCUState`, pure): the most recently active session's line and, from 2, a count. The pill retreats `IslandCUState.linger` (2.5 s) after the last session leaves the list (one wake-up, armed only then).
- **Routing**: the pill hides while its session's host is in front (`IslandRouter.placement` == `.hidden`: its Winter window key/main with Winter frontmost, its exact terminal); a Dispatch child's pill is NOT hidden behind the Dispatch pill (that pill does not say what the computer does).
