# The Winter island

*Read this before touching `apple/Winter/Sources/Island/`, `packages/core/src/island/`, the hello's `capabilities`/`tty`, `island.pending`, or where an approval, question or desktop-switch prompt shows on the Mac.*

A Dynamic-Island-style surface at the Mac's camera housing, in Winter.app. Phase 1: the slim strip, the cards born from the housing, the open island with its tab mechanism and **Needs you**. Phase 2 (below): the tabs — Sessions, Shelf, Usage and limits, Devices, Watches — and the strip's working count ("The tabs"); the computer-use pill and its mirror, and turn-result cards ("Computer use, results, Watches").

## Two forms

- **Cards on top** — quick, ephemeral, born from the housing with the desktop-switch pill's liquid (`DesktopIslandFluid`, `WinterCUPresentation`, made public for this): approvals, questions and URL link requests as **rounded rectangles** (`IslandRectShell` → `CUIslandLiquidFrame` with `DesktopIslandGeometry.bodyCornerRadius`), view requests (the desktop switch, and future ones) as **pills** (the package's own `DesktopIslandView`, same look and words).
- **The island** — the permanent surface. A **slim strip** extending the housing, STATIC. It never covers a menu-bar click it does not need (`IslandLayout.strip(_:waiting:)`): while NOTHING waits it is exactly the housing (black on black, nothing drawn; a click there — where no menu item can be — still opens the island), and on a screen without a housing there is no strip at all (an empty island has nothing to show; this blocks no menu click, which a minimal top-edge target could). While something waits it grows its wings (the housing's width plus 46 pt each side, its height) with the amber "waiting for you" icon on the left and the count of every pending card on the right — or, without a housing, a 112 pt tab hanging from the top centre at the menu bar's height. Only then do the wings or the tab take clicks. A **click** on it opens the island (a black rounded shape growing out of the housing, 520 pt wide, ≤ 560 tall); a click outside (global + local mouse-down monitors) or **Esc** closes it (the panel's own key handler when it is key; a global key-down monitor otherwise, which macOS delivers only to an app trusted for Accessibility). A click on the island's top band closes it too.
- **Tabs**: `IslandTabRegistry.tabs` in swipe order — **Needs you, Sessions, Shelf, Usage and limits, Devices, Watches** (`IslandTabsTests` pins it). A tab is added by naming it there and giving `IslandTabBody` (`IslandTabViews.swift`) a body for its id, plus a refresh in `IslandTabModels.refresh(tab:)`. A **two-finger horizontal swipe** while the pointer is over the open island changes tab (`TrackpadHorizontalSwipeRecognizer`, the Dispatch pill's; fingers left = next, no wrap); small dots show where you are, the selected tab's title on the left.
- **Needs you** lists every pending card, newest first, each answerable there: approvals, questions and links with the transcript's own `TranscriptInteractionCard` (rule options, multi-question decks and notes included), view requests with ✕ / ✓.

## Routing (`IslandRouter`, pure; `IslandRoutingTests`)

1. **Dispatch, or a Dispatch-spawned child** (mode `dispatch`, origin `dispatch-child`, a coordinator among the known Dispatch sessions, or a card mirrored onto one): through the Dispatch pill while it is visible (its floating cards, as before); on top while it is not. The pill's visibility change re-routes.
2. **Any other session**: HIDDEN on top while that session's host is in front; on top otherwise.
   - Winter.app: the app window or a detached window showing the session is key or main AND Winter is frontmost (`AppWindowController.isKeyOrMain`, `DetachedWindowController.isKeyOrMain`).
   - A terminal (the CLI/TUI): only its EXACT terminal window/tab, through its tty (below). No exact match → shown. A phone host (role `remote`) never hides anything.
3. **View requests** (`prompt.desktopVisit`'s card): ALWAYS on top.

Every pending card is in Needs you and the strip's count whatever its placement. Re-routing is event-driven: an app activation, a Winter window becoming key/main, the app (de)activating, the pill showing/hiding, and — only while a card is hidden behind a terminal — an `AXObserver` on that terminal app's focused-window/title changes (`TerminalFocusWatcher`; nothing installed otherwise).

## The terminal match (`TerminalWindowMatcher`, `TerminalWindowMatcherTests`)

- The CLI sends its controlling terminal in its hello (`HelloParams.tty`, `packages/cli/src/tty-name.ts`: the device number of the first terminal among stdin/stdout/stderr matched against `/dev/ttys<minor>`, else a `/dev` scan; no process spawned). The daemon keeps it on the connection's `HubClient` and lists it in `island.pending`'s `hosts`.
- The app reads the process table (`sysctl KERN_PROC_ALL`: pid, parent, `e_tdev` → `devname`, `p_comm`) and, only with Accessibility trust, the frontmost app's focused window title and selected tab title (`AXTabGroup`'s selected tab; 0.1 s messaging timeout, read-only, nothing set) — off the main thread, only when a card's routing needs it.
- The window is read only when a target tty belongs to the front app's process tree — otherwise the answer is "not in front" with no accessibility call at all (which would also switch on a Chromium app's AX tree). Re-judgings are one serial pump in `IslandStore` (fetches and reroutes coalesced; a judgement read while a newer ask came in is read again, never applied — `generation`).
- POSITIVE evidence only: the tty must be on a process descending from the frontmost app (else not in front); the app must have a focused window on this Space (AX, or the window server's on-screen list without trust — minimised, another Space or none → `unknown`); then (1) a title naming a tty names exactly one; (2) in a DEDICATED terminal app (`dedicatedTerminals`: Terminal, iTerm2, Ghostty, kitty, WezTerm, Alacritty, Warp, Hyper, Rio, Tabby) one terminal = that one; (3) there, the title's PROCESS segment (`processSegment`: the one before the size in Terminal.app's "folder — winter — 80×24", or a whole unsegmented title; never the folder) naming the program running in exactly one of its terminals. Anything else is `unknown` → the card shows.
- **Limits**: Terminal.app/iTerm2 titles carry no tty by default, so with several tabs at a shell prompt the answer is `unknown`; iTerm2's sessions are children of its server daemon, not of the app (`unknown`/`notFront` → shown); tmux/ssh: the CLI's pty belongs to a server (shown); an editor's integrated terminal needs the tty in a title; no Accessibility trust → only the process-tree and single-terminal rules.

## Queue (`IslandQueue`, pure; `IslandQueueTests`, `IslandStoreTests`)

- Cards do not stack: a new card REPLACES the current one, which compacts.
- The card being TYPED INTO (its panel took the keyboard for a click in its text field) is held exactly like a hovered one — never replaced, never compacted, never hidden by a re-route — so no keystroke can land in the app behind. The keyboard goes back (`IslandPanel.rest()`, restoring the app the user was in) only when that card is answered (here or anywhere), on an explicit dismiss (a click on the waiting pill) or on Esc; a click in another window simply lets go.
- While the current card is HOVERED or typed into (or is a view request counting down), the newcomer waits as a small compact pill just below it (`IslandWaitingPill`); it expands when the current one clears or self-compacts, or when the user CLICKS it — which tucks the current one away (compacted). A waiting view request is never displaced by an approval.
- **Self-compaction**: approvals, questions and links stay expanded `IslandQueue.selfCompactAfter` (6 s); unhovered they compact into BOTH the badge hanging from the housing ("2 waiting", `IslandBadge`, shown while no card is expanded; hovering it re-expands the newest) AND the Needs-you list. A pointer leaving a card past its time gives it `hoverLeaveGrace` (1.5 s). They stay pending until answered anywhere.
- View requests never self-compact: their deadline (the card's `expiresAt`, default-allow) is the answer.
- A card hidden behind its host leaves the top; when it comes back it goes straight to the badge if it was on top before (no second pop). A card answered from the island goes back up into the housing; one answered elsewhere just goes.

## The desktop switch moved into the island

- The app's hello advertises `CLIENT_CAPABILITY_ISLAND` (`"island"`, `packages/protocol/src/methods.ts`) on its MAIN feed connection (`AppModel.clientCapabilities` → `WinterClient.setClientCapabilities`, re-sent on EVERY reconnect).
- While any island client is connected, `islandAwareDesktopPanel` (`packages/core/src/computer-use/wiring.ts`) does not ask the helper for `prompt.desktopVisit`; the island shows the session's own card (`approval_requested` with `toolName: "ComputerV2"`, `onTimeout: "allow"` and the `switch` option — told apart by that shape, never by its words; `DesktopVisitCardWords` reads the app, bundle id and reason back out of the daemon's sentence only to fill the line). ✓ = `approval.respond {approved: true, optionId: "switch"}`, ✕ = `approved: false`. The first answer wins in the broker across the transcript card, the Dispatch pill, the island and the helper.
- The helper's panel stays the FALLBACK, with the card's own deadline and its answer chained: at a prompt with no island client; when the last island client LEAVES before an answer (a quit or crash mid-prompt — `IslandRegistry.onAllClientsGone`); and when no island client says it shows the card within `ISLAND_SHOWN_ACK_MS` (2 s) — the app sends `island.shown {ids}` for the view requests it puts on top after each `island.pending`, so a Winter.app that is connected but HUNG still gets the prompt on screen (then possibly both show; the first answer wins). `close()` closes whichever is up. `packages/core/test/island/registry.test.ts` pins every leg.

## Daemon (`packages/core/src/island/registry.ts`)

- `IslandRegistry` observes every appended event (`hub.addObserver`): `approval_requested`/`question_asked`/`elicitation_requested` add a card (payload = the event), their resolutions remove it; a Dispatch MIRROR (`childSessionId`) folds into the child's own card (answered at the child); `harness_attached`/`_detached` on a session with a card re-notify. Pending-ness is the BROKERS' (`ApprovalBroker.pendingMeta`, `QuestionBroker.isPending`, `ElicitationBroker.urlFor`): a card the broker no longer holds is pruned at the next list. ≤ 256 held.
- `island.shown {ids}` (harness only): the client shows these cards — the desktop-switch prompt's ack (above).
- `island.pending` (harness role only — not in `REMOTE_ALLOWED_METHODS`, not for plugins) → `{items: [{sessionId, answerSessionId, kind, event, session: {mode, origin, parentSessionId, title}, hosts: [{clientName, role, tty}]}]}`. An island client is told `island.changed` (a bare JSON-RPC notification) once per burst (one microtask): params `{}` when the pending set (or a pending session's hosts) changed — the app then asks again — and `{working: true}` (plus `pending: true` when both happened) when a turn started or ended anywhere (`turn_started`/`turn_completed`): the app re-lists `session.list` for the strip's working count (`IslandCoalescedRun`: one list in flight, one more after a burst). Nothing is sent while no island client is connected; nothing runs while idle. No `SessionEvent` changed: no fixture, no Swift mirror, no remote allowlist.
- `island.hosts` (harness only) → `{sessions: [{sessionId, hosts}]}` for every session with an attachment (`SessionHub.attachedSessionIds`/`attachedHarnesses`): the Sessions tab's "where is it" — `session.list` carries no hosts.
- `island.usage {sessionId?}` (harness only) → `IslandUsageRegistry.snapshot` (`packages/core/src/island/usage.ts`): `windows` — the latest `rate_limit_event` per window kind since the daemon started (the projector's `onRateLimit` dep hands the frame's `rate_limit_info` over; the frame stays unpersisted; the SDK emits it only for subscription-shaped quota, so a home that never hits one has none); `subscription` — `QuotaManager.subscriptionQuota()`; `background` — `quota.state`'s numbers (Winter's background jobs, NOT the user's sessions); `session` — that session's summed `turn_completed` tokens since start (0 for a provider that reports no usage), its last `contextTokens` (sent only for a single-round turn) and its model's catalog `contextWindow` (only for an explicit model tag the catalog knows). Nothing estimated. `packages/core/test/island/usage.test.ts` + the socket leg in `registry.test.ts`.
- Neither is remote-reachable nor a `SessionEvent`: no fixture, no Swift mirror, no allowlist change.
- Swift side: `WinterKit/WinterClient+Island.swift` (`islandPending()`, `islandHosts()`, `islandUsage(sessionId:)`, `capabilityIsland`, `islandChangedNotification`), `IslandClientTests`.

## The app (`apple/Winter/Sources/Island/`)

- `IslandStore` (state, routing applied to the queue, answers through the app's client to the card's ANSWER session — the island never attaches to a session), `IslandController` (panels, pointer, open/close, the one wake-up), `IslandViews`, `IslandLayout`, `IslandPanel` (non-activating, `.statusBar` level, every Space, `sharingType .none`, never key EXCEPT when a click lands in a text field — a question's "Other" — which makes it key for that very click; `rest()` gives it back), `IslandGallery` (offscreen renders). Wired in `AppDelegate.startIsland` (never under unit tests, never without a token).
- Look: the black shell, hairline rim, shadow and morph blur of the Dispatch pill (`CUIslandLiquidFrame`), its type (`Typography.body(.light)`, 14 pt), the white ✕ / blue ✓ circles with `pillHoverGlow`, the 3 % hover grow. Pointer through mouse-move monitors (global + local) installed only while a card, the badge or the open island is on screen; outside what is drawn the top panel ignores the mouse.
- **Screen**: the screen with a camera housing, else the main screen (`IslandLayout.islandScreen`) — a fixed place, re-read when displays change (the helper's own desktop-switch pill uses the pointer's screen instead).
- **Idle**: the strip is a static view; no timer or display link runs. Display-rate frames only while a shape moves (`TimelineView(.animation)` until `DesktopIslandFluid.settle`); the queue's self-compaction is ONE `DispatchWorkItem` while a card is expanded; no ticker for a view request (its expiry arrives as `island.changed`).
- Esc's global key-down monitor exists only while the island is open (removed on close). The view-request pill's VoiceOver label carries no seconds (nothing keeps them current here): "… Winter switches by itself if you don't answer."
- Offscreen renders: `IslandGalleryTests` with `TEST_RUNNER_WINTER_ISLAND_RENDER_DIR=<dir>`; `ImageRenderer` draws AppKit-backed controls (a text field) blank.

## The tabs (phase 2)

The tabs' state lives OUTSIDE `IslandStore` (which stays the cards'): `IslandTabModels` (`IslandTabViews.swift`) holds one model per tab, owned by `IslandController.tabModels` and wired in `AppDelegate.wireIslandTabs`. The controller relayouts on a tab change or a change in the selected tab's content (each tab measures its own flat body), feeds an open-island pointer (rows grow 3 % and lighten, buttons glow) through mouse-move monitors installed only while open, and refreshes the selected tab on open, on a tab change and every `openRefreshInterval` (5 s) **only while the island is open** (one `Task`, cancelled on close) — the Shelf only on open, on a tab change and after a drop, never on the 5 s beat. Closed: no timer, no poll, no monitor. Closing by a click outside drops the keyboard without handing it back (`IslandPanel.letGo()`), so the app the user clicked keeps it; Esc and the top band give it back (`rest()`).

- **Sessions** (`IslandSessions.swift`, `IslandSessionsTests`). Rows from the app's session directory (`session.list`, kept live by its pushes; re-listed while the tab is open — `signals.working` has no push), the island's cards (waiting for you; an armed wake trigger counts too), `island.hosts`, the Dispatch pill's children and the app's own windows. Listed: every non-archived session that is waiting, working, open somewhere (a Winter window, a detached window, a terminal, a phone), marked active/background, or a Dispatch child that finished; never a RUNNING Dispatch child (still the pill's: `ChildPillStatus.isStoppable`, or a working child the pill has not listed). Order: waiting, working, idle, finished; newest first. Host: a detached window, the app window, the Dispatch pill (the Dispatch session), a `cli-` client's tty, a remote client (phone), else "Not open". A **click** (ignored once the island is already closing) closes the island and goes: detached window → `bringToFront`; app window → `summonAppWindow(.session)`; Dispatch → `summonAppWindow(.mode(.dispatch))`; terminal → `TerminalRaiser.raise(tty:)`; nowhere (or a phone) → `openSessionInNewDetachedWindow` — after re-checking the detached windows and the app window (a MINIMISED app window still holds its session and is brought back), so a click never opens a second window on a session.
  - `TerminalRaiser` (pure `ancestors`/`pick`, tested with fakes; the process table and AX reads and actions run off the main thread, only the activation on it): the terminal app is the nearest regular app up the tty's process tree; with Accessibility trust each window, and each tab of its tab group, is judged by `TerminalWindowMatcher.frontness` as if it were in front; only STANDARD windows are candidates (never Preferences or a utility window); with one standard window (≤ 1 tab) the matcher's rules decide, its single-terminal rule included, while with several windows or tabs one is picked only on TITLE evidence (`titleNames`: the title names its tty alone, or its process segment names a program running there only). The one proven gets the AX **raise** action (a tab — only a tab BUTTON, `AXRadioButton`/`AXTabButton` — the AX **press** action) — actions only, no attribute is ever set — and the app is activated. No proof (or no trust): the app is just activated. No app at all (tmux, ssh, a closed terminal): a detached window opens instead. The live raise is not verified on a screen yet.
- **Shelf** (`IslandShelf.swift`, `IslandShelfView.swift`, `IslandShelfTests`). **Paths only, never a copy**: each item is the file's bookmark data (a plain bookmark — the app is not sandboxed, so a security scope would grant nothing) and its last known path, in ONE metadata file, `~/Library/Application Support/<bundle id>/Island/shelf.json` (the dev app's bundle id differs, so dev and dist never share it; never `~/.winter*`). Read lazily on first use, at most 60 items. `resolveAll()` (when the Shelf shows and after a drop; the reads run in a detached task, the results applied on the main actor) follows a move or rename (a bookmark resolves through the file's identity on the same volume; a stale one is re-made) and marks a gone or trashed file **missing** (shown, with ✕). Drops: `IslandHostingView.fileDrop` on the strip's and the open island's panels (`.fileURL`, read with the composer's own `readObjects(… urlReadingFileURLsOnly)`); a drag over the strip/housing opens the island on the Shelf. With no housing and nothing waiting or working there is no strip, so nothing to drop on until the island is open. A drag that starts with a click in another app first closes an open island (the outside-click rule); hovering the strip opens it again. Out: a tile drags as a file URL (`NSItemProvider(object: NSURL)`) to a session's composer or any app; double-click opens it with its default app; the copy button, the menu or **⌘C** put its file URL on the pasteboard — only on that action (a click on a tile lets the island panel take the keyboard, `IslandPanel.takeKeyboard()`, given back on close); ✕ / "Remove from Shelf" never touches the file. Thumbnails: QuickLook (`QLThumbnailGenerator`), requested when a tile appears, the workspace icon meanwhile, cached in memory only.
  - **Screenshots**: the floating thumbnail's drag hands over a file in the screenshot tool's temporary folder (`…/NSIRD_screencaptureui_…/`). When macOS later MOVES it to its final place on the same volume, the bookmark follows (pinned with a temp-file move in the tests). If the file was copied and the temporary one deleted instead, a bookmark cannot follow: an item whose last path is in that temporary folder is looked up by name in the screenshot folder (`com.apple.screencapture` `location`, else the Desktop) — reading only, and taken only when that file was created no earlier than the item was shelved minus `screenshotSlack` (120 s) and has the size recorded at shelving; otherwise the item stays missing and its bookmark and path are not rewritten. A drag that offers only a file promise (no file URL) is not accepted (taking it would mean writing a copy). What the screenshot tool does on a real drag is not verified live yet.
- **Usage and limits** (`IslandUsage.swift`, `IslandUsageTabTests`): from `island.usage` and `settings.modelRoles` — **Plan limits** (a ring per runtime-reported window when it said how full — `utilization` is a FRACTION 0…1, typed after the upstream SDK; anything else shows no fill; the pinned provider runtime's one producer, `quotaEvent`, sends only `status` and `resetsAt` in unix SECONDS — its status and "resets in 2 h 14 min"; a window with no utilization shows a status glyph, never an invented ring; the background jobs' subscription snapshot only when no runtime window of its kind is there); **This session** (the session in front — a key detached window's, the visible app window's, else the Dispatch session's: a context ring + bar only when both its measured context and its model's window are known, and its tokens since start); **Background jobs** (backed off until when, tokens since start); **Notes** (each role's reported problem in Settings → Roles' words).
- **Devices** (`IslandDevices.swift`, `IslandDevicesTests`): **Phones** — the paired phones (`RemoteAccessCoordinator.pairedDevices()`), connected or not (`connectedPhoneIDs()` → `RemoteHost` → `Gateway.connectedPeerIDs()`, the peers with an open connection), "paired N d ago" (the store's `lastSeenAt` is never updated, so no "last seen" is claimed), and a "Phone mirror" row while a phone watches (`RemoteMirrorHub.watchedSessionIds`); **Helpers** — `computerUse.status`: the Computer Use helper (its summary, Accessibility / Screen Recording chips — unknown while it is not running), each browser row (Winter's own browser, Winter for Chrome per Chromium browser with the daemon's reason, Winter (headless)).
- **The strip**: its wings show while something waits OR a session works (`IslandStripModel.showsWings`; idle is still the housing alone): the amber hand and the waiting count on the left wing, a bolt and the working count on the right. The working count is the directory's `signals.working` (not archived), re-listed on every `island.changed {working: true}` (a turn's start or end) and on the directory's own pushes; work that outlives a turn (a background task) ends without a push, so its end shows at the next turn boundary or re-list.
- Offscreen renders: `IslandGallery.renderTabs` (each tab with sample data, the empty and drop-hover Shelf, the strip with counts).

## Computer use, results, Watches (phase 2)

*Files: `packages/core/src/island/activity.ts`; `apple/Winter/Sources/Island/IslandActivity*.swift`, `IslandCUPill.swift`, `IslandResults.swift`, `IslandMirror.swift`; `WinterKit/WinterClient+IslandActivity.swift`. Tests: `packages/core/test/island/activity.test.ts`; `IslandCUPillTests`, `IslandResultsTests`, `IslandWatchesTests`, `IslandMirrorTests`, `IslandActivityGalleryTests`, `IslandActivityClientTests` (WinterKit).*

### Daemon: `island.activity` (`IslandActivity`)
- An observer of every appended event. `island.activity` (harness only, never remote or plugin) → `{computerUse, results, watches}`, each row with the session's facts and (except watches) hosts, exactly as `island.pending`'s. `island.activityChanged` (bare, `{}`, once per burst) goes to the same island clients (`IslandClient.notifyActivity`). No `SessionEvent` changed: no fixture, no Swift mirror, no remote allowlist.
- **computerUse**: a session is listed from its first `computer_v2`/`ComputerV2` `tool_call` in a turn (any thread) to its main `turn_completed` — never per script, so the pill does not blink while the model thinks between scripts. `running` = a script in flight; `title` and `apps` (≤ 3 names: `apps.open` names, bundle ids and `.app` paths shown as names, `browsers.open` hosts) from the latest script; a script naming no app keeps the turn's apps. It ends at the main `turn_completed` or a main `agent_error` (a failed turn, a dead child); an entry with no ComputerV2 call or result for `ISLAND_CU_STALE_MS` (10 min) is dropped, running or not — ONE unref'd timer while anything is listed, which also tells the clients.
- **results**: a main `turn_completed` with `end_turn` and some main-thread assistant text is held `ISLAND_RESULT_SETTLE_MS` (1.5 s, one unref'd timer, only while an island client is connected); a main `turn_started` meanwhile (a queued message, a fold, a loop's next turn) cancels it. Never for `aborted`/`error`, a silent turn, a turn started by an automated door (`ISLAND_NO_RESULT_ORIGINS`: `messaging`, `dispatch` — Dispatch reports its children itself —, `workflow` — a Workflow `agent()` child's `runTurn` —, `routine` — a scheduled routine —, `embedded-probe`), any turn of a `workflow-child` or routine (`routine`, `routine/<id>`) session, or a session with a card still pending. Dispatch's `dispatch-wake` turn and a `computer-wake` turn ARE shown. Summary: markdown marks dropped, one line, ≤ 240 chars. One per session (its next turn removes it), ≤ 32 held, 10 min TTL.
- **watches**: the wake registry's `listAll()` (`ComputerUseControl.wakesList`) — every armed trigger with `armedAt`, labels only (never the model's note); `wake_armed`/`wake_ended` only trigger the notice. Stop = the existing `computerUse.wakes.cancel`.

### The app (`IslandActivityStore`, `IslandActivityController`)
- **One slot under the housing**, its own panel (`IslandPanel`, `.statusBar`, never key, `sharingType .none`), born and retreating with phase 1's liquid (`DesktopIslandFluid`); at rest the shell is `IslandMorphShell` (same black, rim, shadow, 3 % hover grow). **Precedence** (`IslandActivityArbiter`, pure): the open island, or a card needing an answer expanded or waiting on top → the slot is empty (the CU pill and its mirror YIELD — hidden, the mirror's capture stopped — and a result is HELD, born when the top frees within 30 s, else dropped); else a turn result; else the CU pill (or its mirror). With only the badge up the slot hangs just below it and the pill is compact (250 pt). **Rung 4** (the cursor's `foreground` on, until off): the slot is empty and every detached mirror goes alpha 0 and click-through, so the agent's hardware click never meets Winter (the helper's `CUHitTest` skips alpha-0 windows and refuses `winterItself` under any other Winter window). At rest the panel is cut to what it draws plus 22 pt of shadow (a closing mirror's area is kept 0.5 s for its spring), so no transparent Winter area sits over the menu bar or the app below. `IslandController` tells the slot after every redraw of its top (`onTopChanged`) and every focus change (`onFocusChanged`); `IslandQueue`/`IslandStore`'s logic is untouched (the store only holds `activity`).
- **The CU pill** (`IslandCUState`, pure): the most recently active session's line and, from 2, a count. Its words are the agent cursor's own — the helper's `view.cursor` stream, the bubble beside Winter's cursor: `caption` ("Clicking “Publish”" + " in <App>"), `waitBegin` → "Waiting for “<label>”" (the cursor's own caption), type/paste/setValue → "Typing in <App>", key → "Pressing ⌘S in <App>", scroll/drag, rung 4's "Using your mouse"; a press within 1.5 s of a caption keeps the caption. Otherwise (and after `waitEnd`) the latest script's title, else "Using <apps>", else "Using the computer". A change cross-fades with the morph blur (only on change). The app follows the cursor of every listed session through `MirrorCoordinator.setIslandCursorSessions` (a `view.subscribe` WITHOUT frames: bound/released/cursor only). The pill retreats `IslandCUState.linger` (2.5 s) after the last session leaves the list (one wake-up, armed only then).
- **Routing**: the pill hides while its session's host is in front (`IslandRouter.placement` == `.hidden`: its Winter window key/main with Winter frontmost, its exact terminal); a Dispatch child's pill is NOT hidden behind the Dispatch pill (that pill does not say what the computer does).
- **The mirror**: a click on the pill springs it into a 380×300 mirror in the slot (line, detach, collapse, the live picture, the session's title). Pictures are the HELPER's (`view.subscribe` with frames at `MirrorCoordinator.islandMaxFps` 8 / `islandMaxWidth` 640, `Subscription.island`), drawn by the session's own `CUMirrorModel`/`CUMirrorView` (the window panel's model; its pictures gated on `macWantsFrames || islandWantsFrames`). Winter.app captures nothing and needs no Screen Recording; without pictures (the helper lacks the grant, the window is on another Space) the mirror shows its placeholder. `IslandMirrorLifecycle` (protocol seam `IslandMirrorCapture`) starts the frames when the in-island mirror becomes VISIBLE and stops them the instant it is not (collapsed, yielded to a card, the island opened, the session ended). The mirror is bound to the session it was opened on: when the pill moves to another session (recency, its host coming to the front) it closes back to the pill, never showing the other session's pictures. With a phone watching too, the one helper subscription runs at the island's 8 fps; the phone still gets ≤ 5 fps (its relay paces itself).
