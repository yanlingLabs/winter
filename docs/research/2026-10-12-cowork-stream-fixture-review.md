**Required correction: install cleanup ownership immediately after spawn.** `run_stream_fixture.py:52–64` starts the direct child, writes metadata, receives/transfers the peer acknowledgement, starts the oracle, and only then enters `capture_owned_child`. The sole nearby `finally` closes the control socket; there is no surrounding cleanup for the child or peer. In particular, after line 57 sends `A`, the worker may proceed through its thread probe to `krun_start_enter` (`stream_fixture_worker.c:120–128`). A `RuntimeError` from `PeerOracle.begin()`/`Thread.start()`, a setup error, or an interruption before capture supervision then abandons an authorized-to-boot child without a finite kill/wait or a verified exit record. `block_capture.py:47–48` also starts reader threads before its own cleanup `try` at line 60.
