**Descriptor admission and channel separation.** `spikes/cowork-hvf/generic_fixture_lifecycle.py:15` creates the anonymous sampler pipe inside the cleanup guard. Popen inherits only the existing private control socket and sampler writer with `close_fds=True`; the parent closes its writer immediately after spawn. The child receives an owned FD, not a caller-selected sampler path. `generic_sampler.h:12` admits canonical decimal FD numbers in [3,1024), distinct from control/stdio, checks write-only direction and inherited descriptor flags, unlinked FIFO metadata and owner UID, rejects stdout/stderr inode aliases, and restores CLOEXEC plus O_NONBLOCK. These checks run before sandbox/library setup. No sampler descriptor enters the generic stream factory, guest environment or device configuration.

`generic_sampler_emit` formats one record in a 512-byte array, rejects truncation, and writes fewer than 512 bytes in one operation, retrying only interrupted writes. It never falls back to stderr. A separate FD removes the observed C sampler/Rust trace interleaving even when a trace producer writes one line in several pieces. The flood regression uses an actual C helper and intentionally fragmented native trace writes; it does not claim a Rust/libkrun execution.

**Bounds and lifetime.** `generic_capture.py` reuses the existing Limits and direct-child finish implementation. The third channel does not raise the 32 MiB per-channel, 48 MiB total, 64 KiB line, 80,000 row, 64-item queue or 16 KiB chunk limits. Nonblocking readers poll stop every 50 ms. Startup failures, cancellation, overflow, process waits and reader joins remain finite and recorded. Raw stdout, stderr and sampler files remain separate. Malformed JSON-bearing lines are counted/preserved, never repaired; capture completion is not a claim of semantic validity for every line.

Lifecycle cleanup closes untransferred endpoints on spawn/setup failure, retains direct child ownership through peer/oracle setup, and closes stream handles only after reader joins. `samplerClosed`, verified child termination, reader joins and raw-writer join all contribute to `ownersQuiescent`. Uncertain ownership continues to skip disk/raw inspection. A sampler close is not worker termination evidence. No stream/control authority or guest-accessible handle was introduced.
